DATASET DELETE UPDATE — 5 October 2026

Stop ReFlow with Ctrl+C. Back up your project folder first.
Copy the supplied application files into your existing reflow_business folder.
Keep your existing data folder and .env file; neither is included here.
Start: python app.py
Refresh the browser using Ctrl+F5.

Customer: Datasets > Upload history > Delete dataset.
Admin: Datasets > Upload history > Delete dataset, or
AI & Self-Healing > Pipeline run history > Delete source dataset.
Both roles can also delete from the dataset profile.

Read the affected-record counts, then type DELETE to confirm.
Cancellation makes no changes. Deletion permanently removes the selected
source and its derived datasets, linked reports, growth plans and pipeline
runs. Published reports in this group disappear from the customer dashboard.
Other unrelated datasets and business accounts are kept.
Customers can delete only their own business data. Admins can delete any
business dataset. A source delete can remove multiple pipeline runs.

Tested with isolated test accounts and sample data; no real data deleted.
